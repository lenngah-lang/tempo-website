In short

Tempo does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and the app never connects to the internet.

Data on your device

Your settings, the player names you enter, the history of finished games (time controls, move times, dates) and a game in progress are stored only on your device. They are never sent to me or anyone else. You can clear the history in the app at any time; deleting the app removes all of its data.

If you use device backups such as iCloud Backup, this data can be part of your backup. Apple's terms apply to it.

Permissions

Tempo needs no permissions: no access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

App Store

Tempo is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the data needed for downloads and updates (such as your Apple Account) under its own responsibility. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives me anonymous usage statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Contact by email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, technically necessary access data such as your IP address, the date and time, and the page requested are processed in server log files to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This processing can also take place in the United States. There are no cookies and no analytics on this website.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As Tempo stores no data about you with me, these rights mainly concern emails you send me.

Changes

Should a future version of Tempo process personal data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
